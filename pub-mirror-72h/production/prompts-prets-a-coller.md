# Mirror 72H — Les 8 prompts prêts à coller

Chaque prompt est complet : copie tout le bloc dans l'outil vidéo. Format **9:16** pour tous.
Les prompts sont en anglais parce que les outils vidéo les comprennent mieux ; rien de ce texte n'apparaît à l'écran.

**Avant le plan 1 :** génère une image fixe de la fille (prompt ci-dessous) et réutilise-la comme référence de personnage pour garder le même visage.

```
Vertical 9:16 portrait photo, ultra-realistic, shot on a recent iPhone. French woman, 25, natural beauty, light makeup, long brown hair, expressive face, cozy Parisian apartment, soft natural light, chocolate brown and champagne tones.
```

## Plan 1 — Hook (0-5 s)

- Durée à générer : 5 s
- Image(s) de référence : image de la fille générée au préalable (facultatif)

```
Vertical 9:16, ultra-realistic, shot on a recent iPhone, handheld TikTok style, natural skin texture,
soft natural light, French Parisian apartment, premium feminine aesthetic, chocolate brown, gold and
champagne tones. Main character: French woman, 25, natural beauty, light makeup, long brown hair,
expressive face, consistent identity across shots. No text, no subtitles, no logos other than the
reference product.
Product (when visible): the exact tube from the reference image — 100 ml soft squeeze tube, shimmering
metallic plum-brown finish, black flip cap at the bottom, ridged crimp at the top, rectangular front label
with "GLOSS BY K" and "MIRROR 72h" in silvery white and a small gold line of text near the bottom; label
identical to the reference, no distortion. Close-up of the woman's reflection in an entryway mirror, damp coat, she has just come in
from light rain. Her hair is very frizzy, puffy and voluminous from humidity, visible flyaways backlit by
a window. She pulls a strand that springs back, then presses her hair down with both hands and it puffs
back up. Exasperated, slightly exaggerated comedic expression, speaking angrily to her reflection.
Slight handheld shake, quick push-in on her face.
```
